How I built it

Five moves, in order.

  1. Architect the CRM first

    Marketing, Sales and Service Hubs with the full product catalog, integrated with Shopify, OmniSend, GA4 and the ad platforms, so every lead and order had one record.

  2. Launch paid search and paid social

    Google Ads and Meta, judged on ROAS and pipeline in the CRM rather than platform clicks, and tuned month over month.

  3. Build lifecycle from purchase data

    With the product team I modeled how fast customers used up consumables and timed follow-up campaigns to the reorder window.

  4. Close the loop with sales

    I trained every sales and service rep on the CRM and its automations; leadership reviewed the live dashboards weekly.

  5. Put AI inside the CRM

    Named by ZKH's VP of IT and AI to lead a four-person AI team, I designed the HubSpot property mappings that delivered its content to reps.

Hi Lindsay. The posting describes a manager who takes each program from business case through launch, measurement and optimization. This is how I would write the first one: a repeat-purchase program for MyNexeo. It follows the same steps I used to build reorder programs at NorthSky Supply.

01 · Business case

Reorder on MyNexeo

Resin is bought on a cycle. A customer running the same grades month after month is the most predictable revenue a distributor has, and MyNexeo already carries the tools built for that customer: saved favorites, one-click repurchase, live price and availability, order tracking and documents. This program moves repeat orders from existing accounts onto MyNexeo and uses each account's purchase cycle to time every touch.

Target audienceActive accounts with a repeat pattern in the same grades that still place those orders by phone or email.
Desired customer behaviorRegister, save the grades they run as favorites, and place the next reorder online.
Expected financial outcomeHigher retention and order frequency in migrated accounts, added share of wallet, and Account Manager and Customer Experience time returned to selling. Targets are set with Finance before launch.
Assumption to validate firstA meaningful share of repeat orders still arrives offline, and those buyers are reachable by email. The first data pull confirms it or reshapes the program.

03 · Sales and commercial enablement

The Sales play

The program works only if Account Managers see it as a way to protect and grow their accounts. The play gives them a short list, a reason to call and a clear next action.

Target accountsA ranked list for each Account Manager, drawn from the first two segments and ordered by commercial potential.
Cadence
  • Week 0: Account Manager introduction
  • Week 1: invitation email
  • Reorder window: timed reminder
  • Day 30: account review
Routing and follow-upOnline activity from a target account goes to its Account Manager. Unassigned accounts route to SDRs and Inside Sales, order questions to Customer Experience. Response times are agreed with Sales leadership before launch.
Talk track: "You reorder the same grades every few weeks. On MyNexeo they are saved, priced and one click away, with tracking and your documents in the same place."
